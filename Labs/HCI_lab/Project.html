Lab 4HCI · Project

Design a website: from users to a Figma prototype

In this lab your group designs a website with the full HCI process. Seven steps lead you from the benchmark of existing websites to the final PDF and the presentation video. For each step you get the work to do and the sites to use. You decide how to do it: write your results in the answer boxes. Everything you type is saved in your browser.

Deliverable (homework)

One single PDF containing: context and objectives of the website · target users (personas) and their needs · wireframes (low-fidelity sketches) · final mockups of the main screens · the link to your interface prototype (Figma or another tool), shared and opening correctly · your design choices (colours, typography, navigation) · the names of all group members. You also hand in a presentation video (MP4).
