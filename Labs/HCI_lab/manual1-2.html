Interactive manual · Lab 1.2

Three layout managers and the event mechanism, shown on different examples (a mail window, a dice, a doubler). Click in the interface on the left: it reacts like the real program. Then you do your own exercise in the lab.

Objectives

By the end of this manual you will be able to:
  • See how FlowLayout, GridLayout and BorderLayout place components.
  • See how nested JPanel containers build a structured window.
  • See how an ActionListener reacts to a click and how to read a text field.
🔒 The code on the right cannot be selected or copied until you reach the exercise at the end. Read it, understand it, then write your own code in the lab: the example here is not your exercise.
