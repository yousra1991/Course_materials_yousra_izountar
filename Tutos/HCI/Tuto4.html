HCI Tutorial: Interfaces and Interactions with Mockups

GUI and WIMP · Mockups · Interaction design · Accessibility · Creative task

Objectives

By the end of this tutorial, you should be able to:

  • Identify the GUI components and the WIMP elements of a screen, and propose some for a given need.
  • Explain the difference between a static and an interactive mockup, and design a key screen.
  • Describe interaction types (direct, indirect, gesture) and design feedback (visual, auditory, haptic).
  • Check the accessibility of a screen (contrast, size, targets) and improve it.
  • Design and present an interactive mockup of a new feature.

Context · From the problem to the mockup

You continue the redesign of the University of Algiers 1 website. In the previous tutorial your team found the problem (students cannot find information quickly on a phone), built the persona of Sara, and chose three quick wins: a readable mobile layout, a working search, and home shortcuts. Now you design the mockup and define the interaction.

Before you submit

  • ☐ The feature answers a named need of Sara
  • ☐ Exactly 3 screens: Home, task, result
  • ☐ Every button leads somewhere (no dead end)
  • ☐ The main action shows a confirmation (and a way back)
  • ☐ Text 16 px or more, contrast 4.5:1 or more, buttons 44 px or more
  • ☐ Same colours, font and header on all screens
  • ☐ No information given by colour alone
  • ☐ Tested with one classmate and one problem fixed
  • ☐ Video of 30 to 40 seconds, with a voice or captions
  • ☐ Names of the team members on the first screen or in the file name

Deliverables: the prototype (Figma link with view access, prototype.html, or exported PDF), the filled sheets Q15 to Q20, and the video.
