Objectives

By the end of this tutorial, you should be able to:

  • Name and define the 8 ergonomic criteria of Bastien and Scapin and their sub-criteria.

Reminder · the 8 criteria of Bastien and Scapin

1. Guidance

Means to advise, orient, inform and guide the user during the interaction.

  • Prompting: Lead the user towards specific actions and tell them what to enter and in which format.
  • Grouping / distinction of items: Visual organisation of items: by location and by format.
  • Immediate feedback: A quick and informative response to every user action.
  • Legibility: Size, contrast and spacing that make the information easy to read.

2. Workload

Reduce the perceptual and cognitive load and increase the efficiency of the dialogue.

  • Brevity: Limit reading and input effort: conciseness and minimal actions.
  • Information density: Limit the unnecessary information shown at the same time.

3. Explicit control

The system does only what the user asks, only when asked; the user stays in control.

  • Explicit user action: The system acts only after an explicit request.
  • User control: The user can interrupt, cancel, go back and decide the pace.

4. Adaptability

The system behaves according to the context, the needs and the preferences of the user.

  • Flexibility: Several ways to reach the same goal.
  • Users' experience: Respect the level of the user: novice or expert.

5. Error management

Avoid errors, report them well, and allow their correction.

  • Error protection: Prevent errors, confirm dangerous actions.
  • Quality of error messages: Pertinent, readable, precise: what happened and what to do.
  • Error correction: Easy ways to correct a mistake.

6. Consistency

The same design choices (names, formats, positions, procedures) in similar contexts.

7. Significance of codes

Match between a code, a name or an icon and what it stands for.

8. Compatibility

Match with the characteristics of the users (habits, language, expectations) and of their tasks.
