HCI Tutorial 3: Design of HCI

Double Diamond · Discover methods · Define (persona, priority matrix, journey map, information architecture)

Objectives

By the end of this tutorial, you should be able to:

  • Explain the Double Diamond: its four phases, divergent and convergent thinking, and why we iterate.
  • Choose and justify a discovery method (interview, observation, discussion, brainstorming, card sorting).
  • Define the problem: write insights and a problem statement, build a persona, a priority matrix, a journey map and an information architecture.

Context · Redesign of the university website

You are part of a design team in charge of the redesign of the University of Algiers 1 website. Today it is hard to use: students, teachers and administrative staff often cannot find what they need. The team starts a user-centred design process with the Double Diamond.

Throughout the tutorial, the main user is Sara, a L3 ISIL student. The secondary users are teachers and administrative staff.

Q1. Classify each finding by the method that probably produced it.
#Finding collectedMethod
1Sara, a L3 student, said she feels lost and a bit anxious every time she must find her exam timetable or results on the site.
Hint: obtained through a direct exchange of feelings or opinions
2We saw Sara and others zooming and scrolling sideways on their phones because the menus are tiny and the layout is the desktop one.
Hint: gathered by watching real user behaviour
3Administrative staff explained that most e-mails and visits are about the same five questions: registration deadlines, certificates, results, timetable and appeals.
Hint: information reported by professionals during a guided exchange
4During a team session, one designer suggested a single "Student space" with notifications and a chatbot.
Hint: emerged from a creative group idea session
5In an informal talk with a group of students, most said they want the timetable in one click, mostly use their phone, and read Arabic, French and English.
Hint: opinions collected through an informal group talk
6Observation showed that Sara often leaves the site and asks in a Facebook group, because the site search returns nothing useful.
Hint: derived from careful watching without direct questioning
7In a card sorting activity, Sara put "timetable", "exam schedule" and "results" in one group named "My studies", and "certificates", "registration" and "appeals" in another named "Administrative procedures".
Hint: comes from a method used to organise and categorise concepts
8The team proposed a "deadline calendar with reminders", based on Sara missing the re-registration date last year.
Hint: idea suggested internally by designers during creative exploration
9Teachers told us they want to publish course material and announcements in a few clicks, and that today it takes them several steps.
Hint: result of exchanging information with domain experts
